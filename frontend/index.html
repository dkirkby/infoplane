Opening the black box, interactively

A replication of the information-plane dynamics in Opening the Black Box of Deep Neural Networks via Information (Shwartz-Ziv & Tishby, 2017): the authors' tanh network trained on their own 12-bit dataset, with the mutual information of every hidden layer measured at log-spaced epochs across 50 random seeds. Settings follow their released code, which differs from the paper's prose in several places.
