Why the corner marker never moves.

Why individual seeds. The paper's own Figure 2 is a scatter of 50 networks, and the spread is part of what it shows: every seed takes a visibly different route to nearly the same destination. The fitting phase does survive averaging here — all 50 seeds show the deepest layer rising before it compresses, by a median 1.51 bits, around epoch 88 — so the labelled mean markers tell the same story as the cloud. That was not true of an earlier configuration of this pipeline, where the arc was over within two epochs and cancelled in the mean. Bit values hold for the 30-bin estimator; see the Overview.
