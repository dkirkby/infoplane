Figure 2 — the information plane, seed by seed

Every mark is one hidden layer of one network at one epoch: how many bits it keeps about the input, against how many it keeps about the label. Scrub the epoch to watch the layers move right (fitting) and then left (compression). Hover any mark to trace that seed through all seven layers.

epoch 0

I(T;Y) against I(X;T)

350 marks: 7 layers x 50 seeds. Large labelled marks are the ensemble mean per layer.

Why individual seeds. The fitting phase is seed-dependent: 45 of 50 seeds show Layer 7 rising before it compresses, by a median 0.94 bits, but the ensemble mean rises only about 0.49 bits because seeds peak at different epochs and partly cancel. An averaged version of this figure would hide its own subject, so this page plots the per-seed runs and shows the mean only as the labelled markers. Bit values hold for the 30-bin estimator; see the Overview.
