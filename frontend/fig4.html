I(X;T) — bits about the input

Rises during fitting, then falls for thousands of epochs: the compression phase.

I(T;Y) — bits about the label

Rises to the ceiling H(Y) and stays there while I(X;T) is still falling.

Two charts, one shared x-axis — never one chart with two y-scales. I(X;T) spans 0–12 bits and I(T;Y) spans 0–1; putting them on a common axis would invent a relationship between two scales whose alignment is arbitrary.
