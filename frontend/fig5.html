On the theoretical IB bound. The plan for this page called for overlaying the information-bottleneck bound curve. Computing it means solving the IB self-consistent equations for this joint distribution, which the Phase 1 pipeline does not do, so it is not drawn here rather than drawn from a guess. What is drawn are the two limits the data does establish: I(X;T) ≤ H(X) = 12 bits and I(T;Y) ≤ H(Y).

Small multiples rather than four overlaid colours: these are scatter panels, where any two marks can be neighbours, and a categorical palette only clears the colourblind separation floor for three series under that condition. Faceting keeps the validated ordinal layer ramp doing the one job colour has here — depth.
